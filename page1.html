About MUJI

MUJI is a Japanese retail company best known for its minimalist household goods, furniture, clothing, food, and stationery. Founded on the idea of offering well-designed everyday products without excessive branding, it has grown into an internationally recognized lifestyle retailer with stores across Asia, Europe, and North America.

Product for MUJI is philosophy emphasizes simplicity, functionality, and value. Rather than focusing on logos or decorative packaging, the company designs products intended to be practical, durable, and adaptable to many lifestyles. Its catalog spans home organization, kitchenware, travel accessories, apparel, skincare, furniture, and office supplies.

      Design                          Philosophy

              The name MUJI comes from the Japanese                      phrase Mujirushi Ryōhin, meaning "no-brand                  quality goods." The company's design                                approach favors clean lines, neutral colors,                      and thoughtful materials, allowing products to                blend naturally into different homes and                          workspaces. This philosophy has made MUJI                  influential in contemporary product and                            interior design.

1980
The Beginning of MUJI

MUJI was created in Japan with a simple idea: to offer good-quality products without unnecessary branding or decoration.

1980-1990
Simple by Design

Three principles became central to MUJI: selection of materials, streamlining of processes and simplification of packaging.

Now
A Better Everyday

MUJI continues to create simple and useful products while exploring more responsible materials, production and ways of living.
