Store Manager

Job Summary:
Responsible for the overall management of a MUJI store, including daily operations, sales performance, inventory, staff development and customer experience.

Roles & Responsibilities:

-Manage daily store operations and sales activities.
-Lead, motivate and develop store team members.
-Monitor sales, inventory and store performance.
-Maintain MUJI's visual and service standards.
-Support staff training and development.
-Build a welcoming and efficient shopping environment.

Requirements:

-Experience in retail or store management.
-Strong leadership and communication skills.
-Good organizational and problem-solving abilities.
-Passion for customer service and MUJI's philosophy.
-Able to work flexible hours, including weekends and public holidays.

Assistant Store Manager

Job Summary:
Support the Store Manager in managing daily operations, team performance and customer service while ensuring the store maintains MUJI's standards.

Roles & Responsibilities:

-Assist with daily store operations.
-Support staff scheduling and team coordination.
-Monitor sales and inventory.
-Assist with staff training.
-Handle customer enquiries and feedback.
-Support visual merchandising and store presentation.

Requirements:

-Retail experience preferred.
-Good communication and teamwork skills.
-Able to lead and motivate a team.
-Responsible and detail-oriented.
-Comfortable working in a fast-paced retail environment.

Retail Associate

Job Summary:
Provide friendly and knowledgeable customer service while helping customers discover products that suit their everyday needs.

Roles & Responsibilities:

-Welcome and assist customers.
-Provide product information and recommendations.
-Operate POS and handle transactions.
-Replenish and organize merchandise.
-Maintain clean and organized sales areas.
-Support stocktaking and daily store operations.

Requirements:

-Friendly and approachable personality.
-Good communication skills.
-Customer-oriented mindset.
-Team player with a positive attitude.
-Retail experience is an advantage.

Coffee Instructor

Job Summary:
Share knowledge and appreciation of coffee while supporting MUJI's café and coffee-related experiences.

Roles & Responsibilities:

-Prepare and serve coffee.
-Introduce customers to different coffee products.
-Maintain quality and service standards.
-Support staff training in coffee preparation.
-Maintain a clean and welcoming café environment.

Requirements:

-Passion for coffee and customer service.
-Good communication skills.
-Willingness to learn coffee preparation techniques.
-Previous café experience is an advantage.

Human Resources

Support recruitment, employee development and HR operations while helping build a positive and people-focused workplace.

-Coordinate interviews and hiring activities.
-Maintain employee records.
-Support training and development programs.
-Assist with employee engagement activities.
-Support daily HR operations.

Requirements:

-Diploma or Degree in HR, Business or related field.
-Good communication and interpersonal skills.
-Organized and detail-oriented.
-Able to handle confidential information professionally.

Marketing Executive

Job Summary:
Support marketing campaigns and communications that introduce MUJI's products, philosophy and lifestyle to customers.
